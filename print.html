Umrah in 5 steps

1
IHRAM · at the Meeqat

Men: 2 white sheets. Women: modest clothes, no niqab or gloves. Intend and say “Labbayka ‘umratan”, then the Talbiyah until Tawaf.

2
TAWAF · 7 rounds · need wudu

Start and end at the Black Stone line. Ka‘bah on your LEFT. Go outside Hijr Isma‘il. Point and say “Allāhu akbar” each round. Men: right shoulder bare (only in this Tawaf); brisk walk rounds 1–3.

3
PRAY 2 RAK‘AHS · DRINK ZAMZAM

Behind Maqam Ibrahim or anywhere in the Masjid. Recite Al-Kafirun and Al-Ikhlas, one in each rak‘ah. Men cover both shoulders.

4
SA‘I · 7 laps · start SAFA, end MARWAH

Safa → Marwah = 1 lap. On each hill face the Ka‘bah, say “Allāhu akbar” ×3 and make dua. Men jog between the green lights.

5
HAIR · then Umrah is complete

Men: shave, or shorten from all over the head. Women: cut a fingertip’s length (≈2 cm).

What to say

Ihram · Talbiyahلَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ، لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكَ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَLabbayka-llāhumma labbayk. Labbayka lā sharīka laka labbayk. Inna-l-ḥamda wa-n-ni‘mata laka wa-l-mulk. Lā sharīka lak.Here I am, O Allah, here I am. You have no partner. All praise, blessings and dominion are Yours.
Tawaf · Yemeni Corner → Black Stoneرَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِRabbanā ātinā fi-d-dunyā ḥasanah, wa fi-l-ākhirati ḥasanah, wa qinā ‘adhāba-n-nār.Our Lord, give us good in this world and the Hereafter, and protect us from the Fire.
Sa‘i · on Safa and Marwah, after “Allāhu akbar” ×3لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ، وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ، لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ، أَنْجَزَ وَعْدَهُ، وَنَصَرَ عَبْدَهُ، وَهَزَمَ الْأَحْزَابَ وَحْدَهُNone is worthy of worship but Allah alone… He fulfilled His promise, helped His servant, and alone defeated the allies.
⊘ In ihram, avoid

Perfume and scented products · cutting hair or nails · intimacy · hunting · arguing. Men: fitted clothes, covering the head. Women: niqab, gloves.
