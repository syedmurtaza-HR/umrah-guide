When your journey starts
سُبْحَانَ الَّذِي سَخَّرَ لَنَا هَٰذَا وَمَا كُنَّا لَهُ مُقْرِنِينَ، وَإِنَّا إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا لَمُنْقَلِبُونَ
Subḥāna-lladhī sakhkhara lanā hādhā wa mā kunnā lahū muqrinīn, wa innā ilā rabbinā la-munqalibūn.
Meaning: Glory be to the One who has placed this at our service; we could not have done it ourselves. And to our Lord we will surely return.
Qur’an 43:13–14 · part of the travel dua in Sahih Muslim 1342
Remember
Men: practise wearing ihram at homeTry tying the sheets and belt once before you travel.
Expect a lot of walkingSa‘i alone is about 3 km. Break in comfortable sandals before you go.
Avoid
  • Leaving all the learning until you arrive. You will be tired.
  • Packing only scented toiletries. You can’t use them in ihram.
