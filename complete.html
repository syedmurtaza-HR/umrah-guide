ALL 8 STEPS DONEUmrah complete
الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ

Your Umrah is complete.

May Allah accept it from you.

What changes now?
  • All ihram restrictions are lifted. You may wear normal clothes and use perfume.
  • Keep praying in the Masjid and doing extra (voluntary) Tawaf, which needs no ihram.
A greeting pilgrims share
تَقَبَّلَ اللَّهُ مِنَّا وَمِنْكُمْ
Taqabbala-llāhu minnā wa minkum
Meaning: May Allah accept from us and from you.
Farewell Tawaf when leaving Makkah?Required after Hajj. For Umrah, scholars differ. Many recommend it. Ask your scholar.
What comes next?
Visiting Madinah (optional, not part of Umrah)
Going to Madinah?Book a free Rawdah (Riyad al-Jannah) permit in the Nusuk app before you go; you can only enter in your time slot. The Haramain train takes about 2 hours from Makkah to Madinah.

After you return home
Arriving back (added to the travel dua)
آيِبُونَ، تَائِبُونَ، عَابِدُونَ، لِرَبِّنَا حَامِدُونَ
Āyibūna, tā’ibūna, ‘ābidūna, li-rabbinā ḥāmidūn.
Meaning: We return, repenting, worshipping, and praising our Lord.
Sahih Muslim 1342
  • Keep one good habit you built on the trip: a prayer on time, daily dhikr, or reading Qur’an.
  • Tell the people on your dua list that you prayed for them.
