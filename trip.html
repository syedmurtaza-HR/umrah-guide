Just for you
For men
  • Two white, unstitched ihram sheets; head uncovered.
  • Right shoulder uncovered only during Tawaf.
  • Brisk walk in rounds 1 to 3, and a light jog between the green lights.
  • Shave, or shorten from all over the head.

Sahih al-Bukhari 1542 · Sahih Muslim 1218 · Sahih al-Bukhari 1727 · Quick reference

For women
  • Your normal loose, modest clothes; no niqab or gloves.
  • Say the Talbiyah quietly; walk at a normal pace.
  • Cut about a fingertip’s length from the ends of your hair.
  • If your period starts, your Umrah is not lost.

Sahih al-Bukhari 1838 · Sahih al-Bukhari 305 · Sunan Abi Dawud 1984 · Women’s guidance

For couples
  • Intimacy is not allowed until you have both finished Umrah, after cutting your hair.
  • Agree a meeting point, such as the Black Stone line, in case you are separated.
  • A husband may cut his wife’s hair in private.

Qur’an 2:197 · Step 8

With elderly parents
  • Add the condition when entering ihram, in case illness stops them.
  • Tawaf and Sa‘i in a wheelchair or cart are fully valid.
  • Go late at night or after Fajr, and rest between acts.
  • Book their Rawdah permit in Nusuk too.

Sahih al-Bukhari 5089 · Sahih Muslim 1207 · Sahih al-Bukhari 1607 · Care & access

With babies or young children
  • A baby can come without entering ihram.
  • Put on a clean nappy just before Tawaf.
  • No prams on the ground floor around the Ka‘bah; bring a carrier.
  • Print an ID wristband at the machines by the main gates.

Sunan Abi Dawud 4403 · General Authority for the Two Holy Mosques (2024) · Saudi Press Agency (2024) · Families guide

With older children
  • A child’s Umrah is valid, and you are rewarded too.
  • Children who understand make their own intention; you help.
  • Agree a meeting gate and give each child an ID band.
