Umrah at a glance

Umrah has just 4 essential acts, always in this order:

IhramEnter the state
Tawaf7 rounds
Sa‘i7 laps
HairShave or cut

Most pilgrims finish in a few hours, depending on crowds. You can rest between acts, so take your time.

What is Umrah?A short pilgrimage to Makkah that you can do at almost any time of year. It is sometimes called the “lesser pilgrimage”, to tell it apart from Hajj.
Who can perform it?Any Muslim: men, women, children, elderly and disabled pilgrims. Using a wheelchair or getting help from others is completely fine.
Is it obligatory?Scholars differ. Some say once in a lifetime for those able; others say it is a strongly recommended Sunnah.
