Glossary

Simple meanings for the words used in this guide, from A to Z.

Black Stone الحجر الأسود
The stone set in one corner of the Ka‘bah (al-Hajar al-Aswad). Each round of Tawaf starts and ends in line with it. Step 5
Dhikr ذِكْر
Remembering Allah with short phrases, like SubhanAllah or Alhamdulillah.
Dua دُعَاء
A personal prayer or request to Allah, in any language.
Fidyah فِدْيَة
A compensation, such as feeding the poor or a sacrifice, for certain mistakes in ihram. Ask a scholar.
Ghusl غُسْل
A full bath that washes the whole body, for purification.
Halq حَلْق
Shaving the whole head (men). Ends ihram. Step 8
Haram حَرَم
The sacred area around Makkah (and Madinah). Also used for Masjid al-Haram itself.
Hijr Isma‘il (Hatim) حِجْر إسماعيل
The low, curved wall beside the Ka‘bah. It is part of the Ka‘bah, so Tawaf goes around the outside of it.
Idtiba‘ اضْطِبَاع
Men uncovering the right shoulder during the Tawaf of Umrah only.
Ihram إحْرَام
The sacred state of Umrah, entered with intention and the Talbiyah. Also the two white sheets men wear. Step 2
Ka‘bah الكَعْبَة
The cube-shaped House of Allah in Makkah, which Muslims face in prayer.
Mahram مَحْرَم
A close male relative a woman cannot marry, such as her husband, father, son or brother.
Maqam Ibrahim مَقام إبراهيم
The stone where Prophet Ibrahim stood, in a gold-coloured case near the Ka‘bah. Pray two rak‘ahs behind it after Tawaf if there is space.
Marwah المَرْوَة
The hill where Sa‘i ends.
Mas‘a المَسْعَى
The long hall between Safa and Marwah where Sa‘i is done.
Masjid al-Haram المسجد الحرام
The Great Mosque in Makkah that surrounds the Ka‘bah.
Masjid an-Nabawi المسجد النبوي
The Prophet’s Mosque in Madinah. Visiting Madinah
Mataf المَطاف
The open area around the Ka‘bah where Tawaf is done.
Meeqat مِيقَات
A boundary that pilgrims must not cross without being in ihram.
Multazam المُلْتَزَم
The wall between the Black Stone and the door of the Ka‘bah.
Niyyah نِيَّة
Intention, made in the heart.
Nusuk نُسُك
The official app of the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah for permits and bookings. Before you go
Rak‘ah رَكْعَة
One unit of prayer.
Ramal رَمَل
Men walking briskly with short steps in the first three rounds of Tawaf.
Rawdah الرَّوْضَة
The area in the Prophet’s Mosque between his house and his pulpit, called a garden of Paradise. Needs a Nusuk permit.
Safa الصَّفَا
The hill where Sa‘i begins.
Sa‘i سَعْي
Walking seven times between Safa and Marwah. Step 7
Sunnah سُنَّة
The practice of the Prophet ﷺ. Rewarded, but Umrah is valid without it.
Talbiyah تَلْبِيَة
“Labbayka Allahumma labbayk…”, said often from ihram until Tawaf begins.
Taqsir تَقْصِير
Shortening the hair. Ends ihram. Step 8
Tawaf طَوَاف
Walking seven times around the Ka‘bah, anticlockwise. Step 5
Tawaf al-Wada‘ طَوَاف الوَدَاع
The farewell Tawaf before leaving Makkah. Required after Hajj; for Umrah, scholars differ.
Umrah عُمْرَة
The lesser pilgrimage: ihram, Tawaf, Sa‘i and cutting the hair.
Wudu وُضُوء
The washing before prayer. Needed for Tawaf.
Yemeni Corner الرُّكْن اليَمَاني
The corner of the Ka‘bah before the Black Stone (ar-Rukn al-Yamani).
Zamzam زَمْزَم
The blessed well water of Makkah, available all around the Masjid.
