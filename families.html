Umrah with children and babies

Your children are welcome in the Haram. Plan for rest, heat and crowds, and keep things simple. You can do this.

First, decide
A child’s Umrah is valid, and the parent is rewarded. A woman lifted up a child and asked, “Is there Hajj for this one?” The Prophet ﷺ said, “Yes, and you will have a reward.” Sahih Muslim 1336
It is optional for children. Children are not yet required to do acts of worship, so your baby can simply come with you without entering ihram. You do your own Umrah. Sunan Abi Dawud 4403
  1. 1
    Young child: you make the intention for them.

    Dress them for ihram at the Meeqat and intend that this child is entering ihram for Umrah. Then gently take them through each step with you.

  2. 2
    Older child who understands: they intend and do it themselves.

    Help them gently with the words and the counting.

  3. 3
    Ihram rules apply to them as well.

    Keep perfume away from them. Boys: no fitted clothes, and keep the head uncovered. Girls: their normal modest clothes.

Intention for a young child: Shaykh Ibn ‘Uthaymin, as quoted by IslamQA 104345

Babies, nappies & ihram
  1. 1
    Baby not in ihram? Then dress and change them just as you normally would.

    For little babies, this is the simplest choice.

  2. 2
    Baby girl in ihram: her normal clothes and nappies are fine.

    Women and girls may wear stitched clothes in ihram.

  3. 3
    Baby boy in ihram: don’t worry, a nappy is allowed when needed.

    Many scholars count a nappy like fitted clothing. It is allowed out of need, but many say you, the parent, then give a compensation (fidyah): feed six poor people, fast three days, or sacrifice a sheep.

    Scholars differ
  4. 4
    Use unscented wipes and cream while in ihram.

    The same goes for you if you are in ihram.

  5. 5
    Change nappies in the washrooms, never on the Masjid carpet.

    Bag used nappies and put them in a bin outside the prayer area.

  6. 6
    Put on a clean nappy just before Tawaf.

    Scholars differ on how strict purity rules are for a small child. A clean nappy satisfies every view, so you can be at ease.

Praying while holding a babyThe Prophet ﷺ prayed carrying his granddaughter Umamah. But don’t hold a baby with a dirty nappy in prayer; take turns with your spouse instead. Sahih al-Bukhari 516

Nappy in ihram: IslamQA 434494 (Shaykh Ibn ‘Uthaymin); child in ihram: IslamWeb fatwa 210904 · Carrying a baby in Tawaf and prayer: SeekersGuidance (Hanafi)

Tawaf & Sa‘i with children
  1. 1
    Carrying your child? One Tawaf can count for both of you.

    If you intend it for yourself and your child, many scholars say it is enough. Others say do them separately. Follow your scholar.

    Scholars differ
  2. 2
    Try to go at quieter, cooler times.

    Late at night or after Fajr is usually calmer. Try to avoid Friday and the hours around prayer.

  3. 3
    Walk on the outer edge or the upper floors.

    It is longer, but you’ll have room to stop, feed and comfort your child.

  4. 4
    Older children: help them make wudu, then hold hands the whole way.

    Let them hold the counter on your phone, so they feel part of it.

  5. 5
    Stop and rest whenever you need to. There’s no rush.

    Resting between rounds and laps is allowed.

Carrying a child in Tawaf: Shaykh Ibn Baz, as quoted by IslamQA 104345

Prams, carriers & carts
  • No prams on the ground floor of the Mataf (around the Ka‘bah).
  • Prams are allowed on the upper floors of the Mataf and in the Mas‘a (Safa and Marwah), entering through the King Fahd Expansion.
  • When it is very crowded, staff may not let prams into the Masjid at all. So bring a carrier as a back-up.
  • A baby carrier or sling is easiest in crowds. Men may wear one in ihram too; it does not count as clothing.
  • Bring a light, fold-up pram. Wheelchairs and electric carts are kept for the elderly and people who cannot walk. Care & access

  • Inside the Masjid is air-conditioned and can feel cold. Keep a light layer or blanket handy for your baby.
  • Boys in ihram keep their heads uncovered. Keep them shaded with an umbrella instead of a cap.
  • Anyone in ihram needs unscented sunscreen and cream.
  • Give water often, even if they don’t ask for it. Babies: feed them more often in the heat.
Heat warning signs in a childVery sleepy or floppy, hot dry skin, vomiting, or far fewer wet nappies. Move them to shade or air-conditioning straight away, cool them with a wet cloth, give water, and call 911 if they don’t get better quickly.

If a child gets lost
  1. 1
    Before you go in: put an ID band on every child.

    Your phone number, hotel and the child’s name. Wristband printing machines stand at King Abdulaziz Gate, King Fahd Gate and the Ajyad Bridge entrance.

  2. 2
    Take a photo of each child every morning.

    If needed, it shows what they are wearing today.

  3. 3
    Teach them one simple rule: stand still and show your band to a guard.

    Agree a meeting gate too. Save it in My info

  4. 4
    If it happens, stay calm and tell the nearest Masjid staff or security at once.

    Give them the photo and the band details. Emergency: 911.

Ramadan: children’s nursery centresDuring Ramadan, the Two Holy Mosques run supervised nursery centres. You can register online or at the reception desk; each child gets a band matched to the parent’s.

Preparing your children
  • Show them pictures of the Ka‘bah and explain simply: “We walk around Allah’s House seven times.”
  • Teach a few words: Labbayk, Allāhu akbar, SubḥānAllāh.
  • Let them make their own dua, and help them keep a small list of what they want to ask for.
  • Be gentle with yourself and with them. Crying and restlessness are normal, so don’t feel embarrassed. The Prophet ﷺ shortened his prayer when he heard a baby cry. Sahih al-Bukhari 707

Meaning: I seek protection for you both in the perfect words of Allah, from every devil and harmful creature, and from every evil eye.
