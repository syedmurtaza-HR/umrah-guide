Step 02 · Essential

Ihram

الإحرام

The sacred state of Umrah. You enter it with an intention and the Talbiyah. The clothes alone are not ihram.

Where am I?
Before you pass the Meeqat

The Meeqat is a boundary you must not cross without ihram. On a plane, the crew usually announces it. Your group leader will tell you which one you pass.

The five Meeqat
Dhul-Hulayfah (Abyar ‘Ali)Coming from Madinah
Al-Juhfah (Rabigh)Coming from the north-west: Syria, Egypt, North Africa
Qarn al-Manazil (As-Sayl al-Kabir)Coming from Najd and the east
Yalamlam (As-Sa‘diyyah)Coming from Yemen and the south
Dhat ‘IrqComing from Iraq
Already in Makkah?Go outside the Haram boundary, e.g. Masjid ‘A’ishah at Tan‘im, and enter ihram there.

Sahih al-Bukhari 1524, Sahih Muslim 1181; Dhat ‘Irq: Sahih al-Bukhari 1531

What do I do now?
  1. 1
    Get clean.

    Trim nails, remove unwanted hair, take a full bath (ghusl). Men may put perfume on the body, not the clothes, before the intention.

    Sunnah
  2. 2
    Put on ihram clothes.

    See “Men” and “Women” below.

    Must
  3. 3
    Pray, if it is a prayer time.

    It is good to enter ihram after a prayer.

    Scholars differ

    Many scholars recommend 2 rak‘ahs for ihram; others say there is no special ihram prayer.

  4. 4
    At or before the Meeqat, make the intention.

    Intend Umrah in your heart and say: “Labbayka ‘umratan.”

    Must
  5. 5
    Say the Talbiyah often.

    Men aloud, women quietly. Keep saying it until you begin Tawaf.

    Sunnah
MenTwo white, unstitched sheets: one round the waist, one over the shoulders. Head uncovered. Open sandals.
WomenYour normal loose, modest clothes, any colour. No niqab and no gloves. More for women
What do I say?
1 · Intention at the Meeqat
لَبَّيْكَ عُمْرَةً
Labbayka ‘umratan
Meaning: Here I am, O Allah, for Umrah.
Based on the Prophet’s ﷺ wording “Labbayka ‘umratan wa ḥajjan” for Umrah with Hajj · Sahih Muslim 1251
2 · Talbiyah, again and again
لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ، لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكَ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ
Labbayka-llāhumma labbayk. Labbayka lā sharīka laka labbayk. Inna-l-ḥamda wa-n-ni‘mata laka wa-l-mulk. Lā sharīka lak.
Meaning: Here I am, O Allah, here I am. Here I am, You have no partner, here I am. All praise and blessings are Yours, and all dominion. You have no partner.
Sahih al-Bukhari 1549 · Sahih Muslim 1184
Optional · If you fear you may not finish (illness, age)
اللَّهُمَّ مَحِلِّي حَيْثُ حَبَسْتَنِي
Allāhumma maḥillī ḥaythu ḥabastanī
Meaning: O Allah, I will leave ihram wherever You stop me.
Sahih al-Bukhari 5089 · Sahih Muslim 1207
Now in ihram: avoid
  • Perfume or scented soap, cream or wipes
  • Cutting or removing hair or nails
  • Marital relations and intimacy; a marriage contract
  • Hunting animals
  • Arguing, bad language, sins
  • Men: fitted, stitched clothing (shirts, trousers, underwear); covering the head
  • Women: niqab and gloves

Qur’an 2:197 · Sahih al-Bukhari 1542, 1838

Allowed in ihramShowering with unscented soap · changing your ihram sheets · watch, glasses, belt, money pouch · umbrella · bandages and medicine.
Remember
Flying in?Most scholars say enter ihram before the plane passes the Meeqat, not after landing at Jeddah. Some allow Jeddah for certain routes. Follow your scholar.
Made a mistake in ihram?Don’t panic. Things done by forgetting or not knowing are treated gently; some mistakes need a compensation (fidyah). Ask a scholar.
