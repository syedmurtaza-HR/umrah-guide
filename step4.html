Step 04

Entering Masjid al-Haram

The Great Mosque around the Ka‘bah. Enter calmly and walk to the Tawaf area (the Mataf).

Where am I?
At a gate of Masjid al-Haram
What do I do now?
  1. 1
    Note your gate.

    Take a photo of the gate number and name. Put your shoes in your bag.

    Practical
  2. 2
    Step in with your right foot and say the entry dua.Sunnah
  3. 3
    Walk to the Mataf.

    Very crowded? Tawaf on the upper floors is valid too. It is longer, but calmer.

    Your choice
  4. 4
    When you see the Ka‘bah, make dua.

    Ask Allah for anything, in your own language. There is no fixed dua you must say here.

  5. 5
    If the prayer is starting, join it first.

    Then begin Tawaf.

What do I say?
Entering (right foot first)
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ، وَالصَّلَاةُ وَالسَّلَامُ عَلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ، اللَّهُمَّ افْتَحْ لِي أَبْوَابَ رَحْمَتِكَ
Bismillāh, wa-ṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-salāmu ‘alā rasūlillāh. Allāhumma-ftaḥ lī abwāba raḥmatik.
Meaning: In the name of Allah, and peace and blessings on the Messenger of Allah. O Allah, open for me the doors of Your mercy.
Sahih Muslim 713 · Sunan Abi Dawud 465
Leaving the Masjid, left foot first
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ مِنْ فَضْلِكَ
Allāhumma innī as’aluka min faḍlik.
Meaning: O Allah, I ask You from Your bounty.
Sahih Muslim 713
