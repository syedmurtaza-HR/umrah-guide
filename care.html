Umrah is for everyone

Islam makes things easy. If walking, standing or crowds are hard for you, there is a way.

ElderlyDisabilitiesFamiliesFirst-timers
Good to know: these are all valid
Tawaf and Sa‘i in a wheelchair or electric cart. The Prophet ﷺ did Tawaf riding, and told Umm Salamah, who was ill, to do Tawaf riding.
Tawaf on the upper floors or roof.
Resting between Tawaf and Sa‘i, and sitting between laps of Sa‘i.
Skipping Sunnah acts you can’t do: brisk walking, jogging, touching the Black Stone.

Sahih al-Bukhari 1607, 1619

Elderly pilgrims
  1. 1
    Add the condition when entering ihram.

    “Allāhumma maḥillī ḥaythu ḥabastanī.” If illness stops you, you can leave ihram without penalty. See Step 2

  2. 2
    Rest first. Go at quieter, cooler times.

    Late night and early morning are usually calmer.

  3. 3
    Ask for a wheelchair or cart at the Masjid.

    Ask staff at the gates where to find them today; locations and options change.

  4. 4
    Carry water, medicines and a small snack.
People with disabilities
  • Can’t walk? You may be pushed or carried for Tawaf and Sa‘i.
  • Low vision or blind? Go with a companion. This guide works with screen readers, large text and audio.
  • Deaf or hard of hearing? Every dua has written transliteration and meaning; agree hand signals with your group.
  • Can’t cut your own hair? Anyone may cut it for you.
Continuous bleeding or incontinenceMany scholars say make wudu after the time for prayer begins and do Tawaf with it. Ask a scholar about your case.
Families & children
A child’s Umrah is valid, and the parent is rewarded. For a young child, the parent makes the intention and carries them. Sahih Muslim 1336
  1. 1
    Put an ID band on every child.

    Your phone number, hotel name and the child’s name.

  2. 2
    Photo each child every morning.

    So you can show what they are wearing today.

  3. 3
    Agree a meeting gate and teach it.

    “If you are lost, stay still and tell a guard: Gate [NUMBER].”

  4. 4
    Take turns.

    One adult does Tawaf while another rests with small children. Check current rules for prams in the Mataf.

First-time pilgrims
  • It is normal to feel overwhelmed. Stop, breathe, open this guide.
  • Only 4 things are essential. Everything else is a bonus.
  • Walk on the outer edge of the crowd. It is longer but calmer.
  • Use the round and lap counters so you don’t worry about counting.
  • When unsure, ask your group leader or a scholar at the Masjid.
