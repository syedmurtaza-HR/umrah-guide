HELPMistakes & questions
F · Common mistakes

Easy to avoid, once you know. Let's look together.

! Can affect validityEtiquette / Sunnah
! Can affect validity · Step 2
Passing the Meeqat without entering ihram.
Put on ihram before you reach it. If it has already happened, don't panic: ask a scholar straight away (going back, or a compensation).
! Can affect validity · Step 5
Walking through Hijr Isma‘il (the curved wall).
Walk outside it, around the curve. A round through it doesn’t count, so simply repeat that round.
! Can affect validity · Step 7
Starting Sa‘i at Marwah, or counting there-and-back as one lap.
Begin at Safa. Each one-way walk is one lap. You’ll finish at Marwah.
! Can affect validity · Step 8
Men cutting only a few hairs; leaving ihram before cutting.
Shave, or shorten from all over the head. Remember: cut first, then change your clothes.
Sunnah · Step 5
Keeping the right shoulder bare all through ihram, even in prayer.
Bare it only during this Tawaf. When you pray, cover it again.
Etiquette · Step 5
Pushing and hurting people to kiss the Black Stone.
Simply point with your right hand. Kissing it is Sunnah, but harming others is not allowed.
Etiquette
Thinking a special dua must be read for each round, or reading loudly from a book in a group.
Speak to Allah softly, in your own words, from the heart.

Quick questions
I lost count of my rounds.

Don’t worry, it happens to many people. Go with the lower number you are sure of, and carry on.

Can I rest between Tawaf and Sa‘i?

Yes.

Do I need wudu for Sa‘i?

It is recommended, so try to have it. Most scholars say it is not required.

The prayer started during Tawaf or Sa‘i.

Join the prayer with everyone. Afterwards, carry on from where you stopped.

I used scented soap by mistake.

Just wash it off. If you forgot or didn’t know, most scholars say there is no penalty.

Can I shower in ihram?

Yes, you can, with unscented soap. If a few hairs fall out by accident, that’s not a problem.

Must I touch or kiss the Black Stone?

No. Pointing toward it is enough, so don’t worry.

Is Tawaf on the upper floors valid?

Yes.

Can I do Umrah on behalf of my parent?

Yes, you can, for a parent who has passed away or is too old or ill to travel. The Prophet ﷺ told a man to “perform Hajj and Umrah on behalf of your father”. Do your own Umrah first. Then, for theirs, make the intention for them at the Meeqat. Sunan Abi Dawud 1810, 1811 (sahih). Scholars differ on the details

Can I do more than one Umrah on this trip?

Scholars differ here. Some allow it, going out to the Meeqat (often Masjid ‘A’ishah at Tan‘im) for each one. Others advise spending that time in extra Tawaf and prayer instead. Follow the scholar you trust.

Can my child wear ihram?

Yes, of course. A child’s Umrah is valid, and you, the parent, are rewarded too. For a young child, you make the intention and gently help them through each step. Sahih Muslim 1336. Families & children

Can men wear a face mask in ihram?

Scholars differ. Many allow it when there is a need, such as illness or dense crowds; others say men should not cover the face in ihram. Follow the scholar you trust.

Still unsure? That’s okay. Ask your group leader or a scholar at the Masjid. Scholarly notes
