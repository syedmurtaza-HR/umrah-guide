F · Common mistakes

Easy to avoid, once you know

! Can affect validityEtiquette / Sunnah
! Can affect validity · Step 2
Passing the Meeqat without entering ihram.
Enter ihram before. If it already happened, ask a scholar straight away (going back, or a compensation).
! Can affect validity · Step 5
Walking through Hijr Isma‘il (the curved wall).
Walk outside it. A round through it doesn’t count; repeat that round.
! Can affect validity · Step 7
Starting Sa‘i at Marwah, or counting there-and-back as one lap.
Start at Safa. Each one-way walk is one lap. Finish at Marwah.
! Can affect validity · Step 8
Men cutting only a few hairs; leaving ihram before cutting.
Shave, or shorten from all over the head. Cut first, then change clothes.
Sunnah · Step 5
Keeping the right shoulder bare all through ihram, even in prayer.
Bare it only during this Tawaf. Cover it for prayer.
Etiquette · Step 5
Pushing and hurting people to kiss the Black Stone.
Point with your right hand. Kissing it is Sunnah; harming others is not allowed.
Etiquette
Thinking a special dua must be read for each round, or reading loudly from a book in a group.
Speak to Allah softly, in your own words.
Quick questions
I lost count of my rounds.

Go with the lower number you are sure of, and continue.

Can I rest between Tawaf and Sa‘i?

Yes.

Do I need wudu for Sa‘i?

It is recommended. Most scholars say it is not required.

The prayer started during Tawaf or Sa‘i.

Join the prayer. Then continue from where you stopped.

I used scented soap by mistake.

Wash it off. If you forgot or didn’t know, most scholars say there is no penalty.

Can I shower in ihram?

Yes, with unscented soap. A few hairs falling out by accident is not a problem.

Must I touch or kiss the Black Stone?

No. Pointing toward it is enough.

Is Tawaf on the upper floors valid?

Yes.
