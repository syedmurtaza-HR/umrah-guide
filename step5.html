Step 05 · Essential

Tawaf

الطواف

Walk around the Ka‘bah 7 times, with the Ka‘bah on your left, starting and ending at the Black Stone.

Where am I?
In the Mataf, around the Ka‘bah

Anticlockwise. Ka‘bah always on your left.
Look for the green light on the Masjid wall in line with the Black Stone.

What do I do now?
Before you startHave wudu. Stop saying the Talbiyah. Men: uncover the right shoulder (sheet under the right arm, over the left shoulder), only for this Tawaf.
  1. 1
    Stand in line with the Black Stone. Intend Tawaf in your heart.Must
  2. 2
    Touch or kiss the Black Stone only if easy.

    Usually it isn’t. Just face it, raise your right hand toward it and say “Allāhu akbar”.

    Sunnah
  3. 3
    Walk with the Ka‘bah on your left.

    Go around the outside of the low curved wall (Hijr Isma‘il). It is part of the Ka‘bah.

    Must
  4. 4
    Men: walk briskly in rounds 1 to 3, if you can.

    Short, quick steps (ramal). Normal pace in rounds 4 to 7. Skip it in heavy crowds.

    Sunnah
  5. 5
    At the Yemeni Corner, touch it with your right hand if easy.

    If not, just walk on. No need to point.

    Sunnah
  6. 6
    Each time you pass the Black Stone line: one round done.

    Raise your right hand and say “Allāhu akbar”. Tap the counter below.

  7. 7
    After round 7, Tawaf is complete.

    Men: cover your right shoulder again.

What do I say?
At the Black Stone, every round
اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ
Allāhu akbar
Meaning: Allah is the Greatest.
Sahih al-Bukhari 1613. Many also say “Bismillāh” first, reported from Ibn ‘Umar.
Between the Yemeni Corner and the Black Stone
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ
Rabbanā ātinā fi-d-dunyā ḥasanah, wa fi-l-ākhirati ḥasanah, wa qinā ‘adhāba-n-nār.
Meaning: Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire.
Qur’an 2:201 · Sunan Abi Dawud 1892
Suggested during Tawaf: the dua of Ibrahim and Isma‘il
رَبَّنَا تَقَبَّلْ مِنَّا إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ
Rabbanā taqabbal minnā, innaka anta-s-samī‘u-l-‘alīm.
Meaning: Our Lord, accept this from us. You are the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing.
Qur’an 2:127 · a suggestion, not required
The rest of the time: your own wordsThere is no fixed dua for each round. Make dua in any language, remember Allah, or recite Qur’an. More suggested duas
Remember
Prayer called during Tawaf?Join the prayer where you are. Then continue from that point.
Wudu broke during Tawaf?Leave, renew your wudu and come back. Scholars differ on whether you continue or restart. Ask your scholar.
Tired or in a wheelchair?Tawaf in a wheelchair, or on the upper floors, is valid. See Care guidance
Avoid these mistakes
  • Walking inside Hijr Isma‘il. That round does not count.
  • Pushing to reach the Black Stone. Hurting others is a sin; touching it is only Sunnah.
  • Touching the Ka‘bah cloth in ihram. It is perfumed.
  • Loud group duas and stopping in the flow for photos.
