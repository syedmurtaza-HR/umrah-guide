Tawaf

الطواف

Now, let’s walk around the Ka‘bah 7 times, keeping it on your left. We start and end at the Black Stone.

Where am I?
In the Mataf, around the Ka‘bah

Anticlockwise. Keep the Ka‘bah on your left all the time.
To find your start, look for the green light on the Masjid wall in line with the Black Stone.

What do I do now?
Before you startMake sure you have wudu. You can stop saying the Talbiyah now. Men: uncover your right shoulder (sheet under the right arm, over the left shoulder), only for this Tawaf.
  1. 1
    Stand in line with the Black Stone. In your heart, intend Tawaf.Must
  2. 2
    Touch or kiss the Black Stone, but only if it is easy.

    Usually it isn’t, and that’s okay. Just face it, raise your right hand toward it and say “Allāhu akbar”.

    Sunnah
  3. 3
    Now start walking, with the Ka‘bah on your left.

    Stay outside the low curved wall (Hijr Isma‘il) as you go round. It is part of the Ka‘bah.

    Must
  4. 4
    Men: in rounds 1 to 3, walk a little briskly if you can.

    Take short, quick steps (ramal). Walk at a normal pace in rounds 4 to 7. In heavy crowds, just leave it.

    Sunnah
  5. 5
    At the Yemeni Corner, touch it with your right hand if it is easy.

    If you can’t, just keep walking. No need to point.

    Sunnah
  6. 6
    Each time you pass the Black Stone line, that’s one round done.

    Raise your right hand and say “Allāhu akbar”. Then tap the counter below so we keep count together.

  7. 7
    After round 7, your Tawaf is complete. Alhamdulillah.

    Men: now cover your right shoulder again.

Uncovering the right shoulder: Sunan Abi Dawud 1883 · Brisk walk: Sahih Muslim 1218 · Hijr Isma‘il is part of the Ka‘bah: Sahih Muslim 1333 · Touching the Yemeni Corner: Sahih al-Bukhari 1609 · Takbir at the Black Stone: Sahih al-Bukhari 1613

What do I say?
At the Black Stone, every round
اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ
Allāhu akbar
Meaning: Allah is the Greatest.
Sahih al-Bukhari 1613. Many also say “Bismillāh” first, reported from Ibn ‘Umar.
Between the Yemeni Corner and the Black Stone
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ
Rabbanā ātinā fi-d-dunyā ḥasanah, wa fi-l-ākhirati ḥasanah, wa qinā ‘adhāba-n-nār.
Meaning: Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire.
Qur’an 2:201 · Sunan Abi Dawud 1892
Suggested during Tawaf: the dua of Ibrahim and Isma‘il
رَبَّنَا تَقَبَّلْ مِنَّا إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ
Rabbanā taqabbal minnā, innaka anta-s-samī‘u-l-‘alīm.
Meaning: Our Lord, accept this from us. You are the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing.
Qur’an 2:127 · a suggestion, not required
The rest of the time: your own wordsThere is no fixed dua for each round. Make dua in any language you like, remember Allah, or recite Qur’an. More suggested duas
Remember
Prayer called during Tawaf?Join the prayer right where you are. Afterwards, carry on from that same point.
Wudu broke during Tawaf?Don’t worry. Step out, renew your wudu and come back. Scholars differ on whether you continue or restart, so ask your scholar.
Tired or in a wheelchair?That’s fine. Tawaf in a wheelchair, or on the upper floors, is valid. See Care guidance
With a baby or child?If you carry your child, one Tawaf can count for both of you if you intend it (scholars differ). Prams are not allowed on the ground floor around the Ka‘bah. Families guide

Tawaf while riding: Sahih al-Bukhari 1607, 1619

Avoid these mistakes
  • Walking inside Hijr Isma‘il. That round won’t count, so stay outside the wall.
  • Pushing to reach the Black Stone. Hurting others is a sin; touching it is only Sunnah. Please be gentle.
  • Touching the Ka‘bah cloth in ihram. It is perfumed, so keep your hands back.
  • Loud group duas and stopping in the flow for photos. Let’s keep it calm for everyone.
