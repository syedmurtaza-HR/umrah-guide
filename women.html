Umrah for women

The steps are the same as for men, with a few differences. Here they are, step by step.

2Ihram
  • Wear your normal loose, modest clothes, in any colour. There is no special ihram garment for women.
  • Do not wear a niqab (face veil) or gloves while in ihram.
  • Say the Talbiyah quietly.
Covering the face near menMany scholars allow lowering a cloth from the head over the face when non-mahram men are near. Follow your scholar.

No niqab or gloves: Sahih al-Bukhari 1838. The report of women lowering a cloth (Sunan Abi Dawud 1833) is graded weak by al-Albani; scholars rely on other reports.

5Tawaf & 7Sa‘i
  • Keep both shoulders covered. The right-shoulder uncovering is for men only.
  • Walk at a normal pace. No brisk walking in Tawaf, no jogging between the green lights.
  • You don’t need to reach the Black Stone. Pointing is enough. In crowds, the outer edges and upper floors are calmer.
8Cutting hair

Gather your hair and cut about a fingertip’s length (≈2 cm) from the ends, in private. Women do not shave.

Sunan Abi Dawud 1984

If your period starts

Don’t worry. Your Umrah is not lost. Here is what to do.

ABefore ihram? You can still enter ihram at the Meeqat. Take a bath for cleanliness, make the intention and say the Talbiyah.
BBefore Tawaf? Stay in ihram. Do everything except Tawaf. When your period ends, take a full bath (ghusl), then do Tawaf, Sa‘i and cut your hair.
CAfter Tawaf, before Sa‘i? Continue. You can do Sa‘i and cut your hair.
DYour flight leaves before it ends? Scholars give different answers. Speak to a scholar before you travel if you can.

“Do everything a pilgrim does, except Tawaf of the House, until you are clean.” Sahih al-Bukhari 305. Ihram after childbirth bleeding: Sahih Muslim 1209.

Staying inside the Masjid during a periodMost scholars say don’t stay in the Masjid; some allow it. Follow your scholar.
Medicine to delay a periodMany scholars permit it if it is safe for you. Ask your doctor first.
Travelling
Travelling without a mahramSaudi rules: since October 2022 the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah no longer requires women to have a mahram for Umrah. Religious ruling: scholars differ. Ask your scholar.
