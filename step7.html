Step 07 · Essential

Sa‘i

السعي

Now let’s walk between the hills of Safa and Marwah 7 times. Start at Safa, finish at Marwah. It’s about 3 km in all, so go at your own pace.

Where am I?
The Mas‘a: the long hall of Safa and Marwah
What do I do now?
  1. 1
    Go to Safa. As you get close, recite the verse (first time only).Sunnah
  2. 2
    Once on Safa, face the Ka‘bah and raise your hands.

    Say “Allāhu akbar” three times and the dhikr below, then make your own dua. Do this three times.

    Sunnah
  3. 3
    Now walk to Marwah. That is lap 1.Must: 7 laps, Safa first
  4. 4
    Men: jog lightly between the green lights.

    Women walk normally the whole way.

    Sunnah
  5. 5
    On Marwah, face the Ka‘bah and do the same as you did on Safa.

    (Not the verse.) Then walk back to Safa: that’s lap 2.

  6. 6
    Lap 7 ends at Marwah. Your Sa‘i is complete.

What do I say?
Approaching Safa, first lap only
إِنَّ الصَّفَا وَالْمَرْوَةَ مِنْ شَعَائِرِ اللَّهِ ۝ أَبْدَأُ بِمَا بَدَأَ اللَّهُ بِهِ
Inna-ṣ-Ṣafā wa-l-Marwata min sha‘ā’iri-llāh. Abda’u bimā bada’a-llāhu bih.
Meaning: Indeed Safa and Marwah are among the signs of Allah. I begin with what Allah began with.
Qur’an 2:158 · Sahih Muslim 1218
On Safa and on Marwah, facing the Ka‘bah
اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ. لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ، وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ، لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ، أَنْجَزَ وَعْدَهُ، وَنَصَرَ عَبْدَهُ، وَهَزَمَ الْأَحْزَابَ وَحْدَهُ
Allāhu akbar (×3). Lā ilāha illa-llāhu waḥdahū lā sharīka lah, lahu-l-mulku wa lahu-l-ḥamd, wa huwa ‘alā kulli shay’in qadīr. Lā ilāha illa-llāhu waḥdah, anjaza wa‘dah, wa naṣara ‘abdah, wa hazama-l-aḥzāba waḥdah.
Meaning: Allah is the Greatest. None has the right to be worshipped but Allah alone, without partner. His is the dominion and the praise, and He has power over all things. None has the right to be worshipped but Allah alone. He fulfilled His promise, helped His servant, and alone defeated the allied forces.
Sahih Muslim 1218 · say it 3 times, with your own dua in between
Between the hills (reported from the Companions)
رَبِّ اغْفِرْ وَارْحَمْ، إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ الْأَعَزُّ الْأَكْرَمُ
Rabbi-ghfir warḥam, innaka anta-l-a‘azzu-l-akram.
Meaning: My Lord, forgive and have mercy. You are the Most Mighty, the Most Generous.
Reported from Companions, not from the Prophet ﷺ · Musannaf Ibn Abi Shaybah; al-Bayhaqi 5/95
While walking: your own wordsNo fixed dua is required between the hills. Make dua, dhikr or recite Qur’an, whatever is in your heart. Ideas for dhikr
Remember
You may rest and drinkIf you need to, sit down between laps. That’s okay. Wudu is recommended for Sa‘i, but most scholars say it is not required.
Wheelchair?No problem. Sa‘i in a wheelchair is valid. There are wheelchair lanes and upper floors. Care guidance
With a baby or child?Prams are allowed in the Mas‘a (enter through the King Fahd Expansion). Families guide

Sa‘i without wudu: most scholars, based on Sahih al-Bukhari 305 · Riding: Sahih al-Bukhari 1607

Avoid these mistakes
  • Starting at Marwah. That first lap won’t count. Always begin at Safa.
  • Counting there-and-back as one lap. Safa → Marwah is one; Marwah → Safa is the next.
  • Running the whole way. The light jog is only for men, only between the green lights.
