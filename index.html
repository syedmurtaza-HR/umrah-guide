What is Umrah?A short pilgrimage to Makkah that can be done at almost any time of year. It is sometimes called the “lesser pilgrimage”, to tell it apart from Hajj.
Who can perform it?Any Muslim: men, women, children, elderly and disabled pilgrims. A wheelchair or help from others is fine.
Is it obligatory?Scholars differ. Some say once in a lifetime for those able; others say it is a strongly recommended Sunnah.
