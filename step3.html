Step 03

Entering Makkah

مكة

You are in ihram now. Keep saying the Talbiyah, settle in, and let’s get ready for the Masjid together.

Where am I?
On the way to Makkah / at your hotel
What do I do now?
  1. 1
    Keep saying the Talbiyah on the way.Sunnah
  2. 2
    Check in and rest if you need to. There’s no rush.

    It is fine to sleep or eat before Tawaf. When you are rested, you make fewer mistakes.

    Optional
  3. 3
    Take a bath if easy, then make wudu.

    The Prophet ﷺ bathed before entering Makkah. Wudu is needed for Tawaf.

    Wudu: must for Tawaf
  4. 4
    Agree a meeting point with your group, in case you get separated.

    Pick a Masjid gate by its number and name, and write it in My info so you don’t forget.

    Practical
  5. 5
    Take only what you need, and keep it light.

    Water, phone, charger, a bag for your shoes, this guide.

Talbiyah until the Haram, and bathing before Makkah: Sahih al-Bukhari 1573 · Wudu before Tawaf: Sahih al-Bukhari 1614

What do I say?
Talbiyah, until you begin Tawaf
لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ، لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكَ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ
Labbayka-llāhumma labbayk. Labbayka lā sharīka laka labbayk. Inna-l-ḥamda wa-n-ni‘mata laka wa-l-mulk. Lā sharīka lak.
Meaning: Here I am, O Allah, here I am. Here I am, You have no partner, here I am. All praise and blessings are Yours, and all dominion. You have no partner.
Sahih al-Bukhari 1549 · Sahih Muslim 1184
Arriving at your hotel (a new place)
أَعُوذُ بِكَلِمَاتِ اللَّهِ التَّامَّاتِ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ
A‘ūdhu bi-kalimāti-llāhi-t-tāmmāti min sharri mā khalaq.
Meaning: I seek refuge in the perfect words of Allah from the evil of what He has created.
Sahih Muslim 2708

You can also remember Allah in your own words, in any language. He hears you.

Remember
Patience is part of your UmrahThere will be crowds, heat and long walks. Be gentle with others, and with yourself too.
AvoidLeaving the hotel without knowing how to get back. Take a quick photo of the hotel name and street before you go.

Bath before entering Makkah: Sahih al-Bukhari 1573
