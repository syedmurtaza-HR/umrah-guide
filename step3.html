Step 03

Entering Makkah

مكة

You are in ihram now. Keep saying the Talbiyah, settle in, and get ready for the Masjid.

Where am I?
On the way to Makkah / at your hotel
What do I do now?
  1. 1
    Keep saying the Talbiyah on the way.Sunnah
  2. 2
    Check in and rest if you need to.

    It is fine to sleep or eat before Tawaf. A rested pilgrim makes fewer mistakes.

    Optional
  3. 3
    Take a bath if easy, then make wudu.

    The Prophet ﷺ bathed before entering Makkah. Wudu is needed for Tawaf.

    Wudu: must for Tawaf
  4. 4
    Agree a meeting point with your group.

    Pick a Masjid gate by its number and name. Write it in My info.

    Practical
  5. 5
    Take only what you need.

    Water, phone, charger, a bag for your shoes, this guide.

What do I say?
The TalbiyahLabbayka-llāhumma labbayk… Tap to see it in full.

You can also remember Allah in your own words, in any language.

Remember
Patience is part of your UmrahExpect crowds, heat and long walks. Be gentle with others and with yourself.
AvoidLeaving the hotel without knowing how to get back. Take a photo of the hotel name and street.

Bath before entering Makkah: Sahih al-Bukhari 1573
