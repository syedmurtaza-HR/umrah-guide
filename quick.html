1IHRAM

At the Meeqat: intend Umrah. Men in 2 white sheets.

“Labbayka ‘umratan” · then the Talbiyah 2TAWAF · 7 rounds

Start and end at the Black Stone. Ka‘bah on your left. Outside Hijr Isma‘il. Need wudu.

“Allāhu akbar” at the Black Stone · “Rabbanā ātinā…” before it 3PRAY 2 · ZAMZAM

2 rak‘ahs behind Maqam Ibrahim or anywhere. Drink Zamzam, make dua.

 4SA‘I · 7 laps

Start Safa, end Marwah. Safa → Marwah = 1 lap. Men jog between green lights.

On each hill: face the Ka‘bah, “Allāhu akbar” ×3, dhikr, dua 5HAIR

Men: shave, or shorten all over. Women: fingertip length.
