Visiting Madinah

المدينة

Most pilgrims also visit the Prophet’s Mosque in Madinah. It is a separate, blessed visit. Your Umrah is complete without it.

Where am I?
Madinah, at Masjid an-Nabawi
Why visit“One prayer in my Mosque is better than a thousand prayers in any other mosque, except al-Masjid al-Haram.” Sahih al-Bukhari 1190. It is one of only three mosques the Prophet ﷺ encouraged travelling to. Sahih al-Bukhari 1189
What do I do?
  1. 1
    Enter the Masjid with your right foot and say the entry dua.

    The same dua as in Makkah. No ihram is needed in Madinah.

    Sunnah
  2. 2
    Pray two rak‘ahs to greet the Masjid.

    As you would in any mosque, if it is not a prayer time.

    Sunnah
  3. 3
    Pray in the Rawdah, with your Nusuk permit.

    “Between my house and my pulpit is a garden of the gardens of Paradise.” Enter only in your booked time slot. Men and women have separate times.

    Permit needed
  4. 4
    Greet the Prophet ﷺ, then Abu Bakr and ‘Umar.

    Walk calmly along the grave area, say salam softly, and keep moving with the line.

  5. 5
    Visit Masjid Quba.

    Make wudu where you are staying, then go and pray there. The Prophet ﷺ said this carries a reward like that of an Umrah.

    Sunnah
  6. 6
    Visit al-Baqi‘ and the martyrs of Uhud, if you wish.

    Greet those buried there with the graveyard dua below, and pray for them.

    Optional

Rawdah: Sahih al-Bukhari 1196 · Quba: Sunan Ibn Majah 1412 (hasan)

What do I say?
Greeting at the grave of the Prophet ﷺ
السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكَ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكَ يَا أَبَا بَكْرٍ، السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكَ يَا عُمَرُ
As-salāmu ‘alayka yā Rasūla-llāh. As-salāmu ‘alayka yā Abā Bakr. As-salāmu ‘alayka yā ‘Umar.
Meaning: Peace be upon you, O Messenger of Allah. Peace be upon you, O Abu Bakr. Peace be upon you, O ‘Umar.
The greeting of Ibn ‘Umar, who said “O my father” for ‘Umar · al-Bayhaqi, al-Sunan al-Kubra 10271
Visiting al-Baqi‘ or any graveyard
السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ أَهْلَ الدِّيَارِ مِنَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ وَالْمُسْلِمِينَ، وَإِنَّا إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ لَلَاحِقُونَ، أَسْأَلُ اللَّهَ لَنَا وَلَكُمُ الْعَافِيَةَ
As-salāmu ‘alaykum ahla-d-diyāri mina-l-mu’minīna wa-l-muslimīn, wa innā in shā’a-llāhu la-lāḥiqūn, as’alu-llāha lanā wa lakumu-l-‘āfiyah.
Meaning: Peace be upon you, O believers and Muslims who dwell here. We will, if Allah wills, join you. I ask Allah for well-being for us and for you.
Sahih Muslim 975
Send blessings on the Prophet ﷺ oftenThe full Salawat is in Duas & Dhikr.
Remember
Going from Madinah to Makkah for Umrah?Enter ihram at the Madinah Meeqat, Dhul-Hulayfah (Abyar ‘Ali), before you continue. The Haramain train takes about 2 hours.
Rawdah permitBook it in the Nusuk app before you arrive. You cannot enter outside your slot.
Women visiting graveyardsScholars differ on this. Follow your scholar, and check the current visiting arrangements on site.
Avoid
  • Raising your voice in the Masjid, especially near the grave.
  • Pushing in the Rawdah or at the grave. Give others space; everyone has limited time.
  • Stopping in the walkways and blocking the line.
