B · Journey map

8 steps. Let’s start with step 1.

Tap any step to open it. Steps with the Essential label are the 4 acts every Umrah must include.

01Before Umrah

Sort your papers, pack, and learn the steps.

 02Ihram

At the Meeqat, enter the sacred state and say the Talbiyah.

Essential 03Entering Makkah

Check in, rest a little, make wudu.

 04Entering Masjid al-Haram

Step in right foot first, then go to the Tawaf area.

 05Tawaf

7 rounds around the Ka‘bah, Ka‘bah on your left.

Essential 06After Tawaf

Pray 2 rak‘ahs, then drink Zamzam.

 07Sa‘i

7 laps between Safa and Marwah, starting at Safa.

Essential 08Halq / Taqsir

Shave or shorten the hair. This ends ihram.

Essential Umrah complete

Ihram restrictions are lifted. Alhamdulillah, you did it.
A note on “Essential”Schools classify these acts differently (pillar or obligation), but every pilgrim performs all four. Read the notes
