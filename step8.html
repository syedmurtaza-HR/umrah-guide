Step 08 · Essential · Last step

Shave or shorten your hair

This is the last act of Umrah. It ends your ihram. Halq = shave. Taqsir = shorten.

Where am I?
After Sa‘i: barbers near the Marwah exits, or your hotel
What do I do now?
MenMust
  1. A
    Shave the whole head.

    This is better: the Prophet ﷺ made dua three times for those who shave.

    Best
  2. B
    Or shorten hair from all over the head.

    Not just a few strands from one side.

    Scholars differ on the minimum
WomenMust
  1. 1
    Gather your hair together.

    In private: at your hotel, by yourself, a mahram, or another woman.

  2. 2
    Cut about a fingertip’s length from the ends.

    Roughly 2 cm. Women do not shave.

What do I say?

Nothing specific is required. Thank Allah in your own words.

A simple word of thanks
الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ
Al-ḥamdu lillāh
Meaning: All praise is for Allah.
Remember
Use a new, clean bladeAsk the barber to open a fresh blade in front of you.
AvoidChanging out of ihram clothes, or using perfume, before your hair is cut.

Dua for those who shave: Sahih al-Bukhari 1727, Sahih Muslim 1301 · Women shorten, not shave: Sunan Abi Dawud 1984
