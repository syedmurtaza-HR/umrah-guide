D · Organised by moment

What to say, and when

You may always use your own wordsDua can be made in any language. Only a few moments have a wording from the Sunnah, shown below.
1Entering ihram
Intention, at the Meeqat
لَبَّيْكَ عُمْرَةً
Labbayka ‘umratan
Meaning: Here I am, O Allah, for Umrah.
Based on the Prophet’s ﷺ wording “Labbayka ‘umratan wa ḥajjan” for Umrah with Hajj · Sahih Muslim 1251
Talbiyah, until Tawaf begins
لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ، لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكَ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ
Labbayka-llāhumma labbayk. Labbayka lā sharīka laka labbayk. Inna-l-ḥamda wa-n-ni‘mata laka wa-l-mulk. Lā sharīka lak.
Meaning: Here I am, O Allah, here I am. Here I am, You have no partner, here I am. All praise and blessings are Yours, and all dominion. You have no partner.
Sahih al-Bukhari 1549 · Sahih Muslim 1184
Optional condition, if you fear you can’t finish
اللَّهُمَّ مَحِلِّي حَيْثُ حَبَسْتَنِي
Allāhumma maḥillī ḥaythu ḥabastanī
Meaning: O Allah, I will leave ihram wherever You stop me.
Sahih al-Bukhari 5089 · Sahih Muslim 1207
2Entering & leaving the Masjid
Entering, right foot first
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ، وَالصَّلَاةُ وَالسَّلَامُ عَلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ، اللَّهُمَّ افْتَحْ لِي أَبْوَابَ رَحْمَتِكَ
Bismillāh, wa-ṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-salāmu ‘alā rasūlillāh. Allāhumma-ftaḥ lī abwāba raḥmatik.
Meaning: In the name of Allah, and peace and blessings on the Messenger of Allah. O Allah, open for me the doors of Your mercy.
Sahih Muslim 713 · Sunan Abi Dawud 465
Leaving, left foot first
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ مِنْ فَضْلِكَ
Allāhumma innī as’aluka min faḍlik.
Meaning: O Allah, I ask You from Your bounty.
Sahih Muslim 713
3During Tawaf
At the Black Stone, each round
اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ
Allāhu akbar
Meaning: Allah is the Greatest.
Sahih al-Bukhari 1613
Yemeni Corner → Black Stone
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ
Rabbanā ātinā fi-d-dunyā ḥasanah, wa fi-l-ākhirati ḥasanah, wa qinā ‘adhāba-n-nār.
Meaning: Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire.
Qur’an 2:201 · Sunan Abi Dawud 1892

For the rest of each round there is no fixed dua. Use the dhikr at the bottom of this page or your own words.

4After Tawaf
Going to Maqam Ibrahim
وَاتَّخِذُوا مِنْ مَقَامِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ مُصَلًّى
Wattakhidhū min maqāmi Ibrāhīma muṣallā.
Meaning: And take the standing place of Ibrahim as a place of prayer.
Qur’an 2:125 · Sahih Muslim 1218
ZamzamAny dua, in your own words. “Zamzam water is for whatever it is drunk for.” Sunan Ibn Majah 3062
5Safa & Marwah
Approaching Safa, first time only
إِنَّ الصَّفَا وَالْمَرْوَةَ مِنْ شَعَائِرِ اللَّهِ ۝ أَبْدَأُ بِمَا بَدَأَ اللَّهُ بِهِ
Inna-ṣ-Ṣafā wa-l-Marwata min sha‘ā’iri-llāh. Abda’u bimā bada’a-llāhu bih.
Meaning: Indeed Safa and Marwah are among the signs of Allah. I begin with what Allah began with.
Qur’an 2:158 · Sahih Muslim 1218
On each hill, facing the Ka‘bah (×3)
لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ، وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ، لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ، أَنْجَزَ وَعْدَهُ، وَنَصَرَ عَبْدَهُ، وَهَزَمَ الْأَحْزَابَ وَحْدَهُ
Lā ilāha illa-llāhu waḥdahū lā sharīka lah, lahu-l-mulku wa lahu-l-ḥamd, wa huwa ‘alā kulli shay’in qadīr. Lā ilāha illa-llāhu waḥdah, anjaza wa‘dah, wa naṣara ‘abdah, wa hazama-l-aḥzāba waḥdah.
Meaning: None has the right to be worshipped but Allah alone, without partner. His is the dominion and the praise, and He has power over all things. None has the right to be worshipped but Allah alone. He fulfilled His promise, helped His servant, and alone defeated the allied forces.
Sahih Muslim 1218 · begin with “Allāhu akbar” ×3
Between the hills (from the Companions)
رَبِّ اغْفِرْ وَارْحَمْ، إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ الْأَعَزُّ الْأَكْرَمُ
Rabbi-ghfir warḥam, innaka anta-l-a‘azzu-l-akram.
Meaning: My Lord, forgive and have mercy. You are the Most Mighty, the Most Generous.
Reported from Companions, not from the Prophet ﷺ · Musannaf Ibn Abi Shaybah (Book of Hajj); al-Bayhaqi, al-Sunan al-Kubra 5/95
