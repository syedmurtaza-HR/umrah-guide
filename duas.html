D · Organised by moment

What to say, and when

You can always use your own wordsYou can make dua in any language. Only a few moments have a set wording from the Sunnah, and I’ve shown them below.
My dua listKeep the names and requests of everyone who asked you to pray for them, and tick them off in Tawaf.
1Entering ihram
Intention, at the Meeqat
لَبَّيْكَ عُمْرَةً
Labbayka ‘umratan
Meaning: Here I am, O Allah, for Umrah.
Based on the Prophet’s ﷺ wording “Labbayka ‘umratan wa ḥajjan” for Umrah with Hajj · Sahih Muslim 1251
Talbiyah, until Tawaf begins
لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ، لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكَ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ
Labbayka-llāhumma labbayk. Labbayka lā sharīka laka labbayk. Inna-l-ḥamda wa-n-ni‘mata laka wa-l-mulk. Lā sharīka lak.
Meaning: Here I am, O Allah, here I am. Here I am, You have no partner, here I am. All praise and blessings are Yours, and all dominion. You have no partner.
Sahih al-Bukhari 1549 · Sahih Muslim 1184
Optional condition, if you fear you can’t finish
اللَّهُمَّ مَحِلِّي حَيْثُ حَبَسْتَنِي
Allāhumma maḥillī ḥaythu ḥabastanī
Meaning: O Allah, I will leave ihram wherever You stop me.
Sahih al-Bukhari 5089 · Sahih Muslim 1207
2Entering & leaving the Masjid
Entering, right foot first
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ، وَالصَّلَاةُ وَالسَّلَامُ عَلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ، اللَّهُمَّ افْتَحْ لِي أَبْوَابَ رَحْمَتِكَ
Bismillāh, wa-ṣ-ṣalātu wa-s-salāmu ‘alā rasūlillāh. Allāhumma-ftaḥ lī abwāba raḥmatik.
Meaning: In the name of Allah, and peace and blessings on the Messenger of Allah. O Allah, open for me the doors of Your mercy.
Sahih Muslim 713 · Sunan Abi Dawud 465
Leaving, left foot first
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ مِنْ فَضْلِكَ
Allāhumma innī as’aluka min faḍlik.
Meaning: O Allah, I ask You from Your bounty.
Sahih Muslim 713
3During Tawaf
At the Black Stone, each round
اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ
Allāhu akbar
Meaning: Allah is the Greatest.
Sahih al-Bukhari 1613
Yemeni Corner → Black Stone
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ
Rabbanā ātinā fi-d-dunyā ḥasanah, wa fi-l-ākhirati ḥasanah, wa qinā ‘adhāba-n-nār.
Meaning: Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire.
Qur’an 2:201 · Sunan Abi Dawud 1892

For the rest of each round there is no fixed dua. Use the dhikr at the bottom of this page, or simply speak to Allah in your own words.

4After Tawaf
Going to Maqam Ibrahim
وَاتَّخِذُوا مِنْ مَقَامِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ مُصَلًّى
Wattakhidhū min maqāmi Ibrāhīma muṣallā.
Meaning: And take the standing place of Ibrahim as a place of prayer.
Qur’an 2:125 · Sahih Muslim 1218
ZamzamMake any dua you like, in your own words. “Zamzam water is for whatever it is drunk for.” Sunan Ibn Majah 3062
5Safa & Marwah
Approaching Safa, first time only
إِنَّ الصَّفَا وَالْمَرْوَةَ مِنْ شَعَائِرِ اللَّهِ ۝ أَبْدَأُ بِمَا بَدَأَ اللَّهُ بِهِ
Inna-ṣ-Ṣafā wa-l-Marwata min sha‘ā’iri-llāh. Abda’u bimā bada’a-llāhu bih.
Meaning: Indeed Safa and Marwah are among the signs of Allah. I begin with what Allah began with.
Qur’an 2:158 · Sahih Muslim 1218
On each hill, facing the Ka‘bah (×3)
لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ، وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ، لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ، أَنْجَزَ وَعْدَهُ، وَنَصَرَ عَبْدَهُ، وَهَزَمَ الْأَحْزَابَ وَحْدَهُ
Lā ilāha illa-llāhu waḥdahū lā sharīka lah, lahu-l-mulku wa lahu-l-ḥamd, wa huwa ‘alā kulli shay’in qadīr. Lā ilāha illa-llāhu waḥdah, anjaza wa‘dah, wa naṣara ‘abdah, wa hazama-l-aḥzāba waḥdah.
Meaning: None has the right to be worshipped but Allah alone, without partner. His is the dominion and the praise, and He has power over all things. None has the right to be worshipped but Allah alone. He fulfilled His promise, helped His servant, and alone defeated the allied forces.
Sahih Muslim 1218 · begin with “Allāhu akbar” ×3
Between the hills (from the Companions)
رَبِّ اغْفِرْ وَارْحَمْ، إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ الْأَعَزُّ الْأَكْرَمُ
Rabbi-ghfir warḥam, innaka anta-l-a‘azzu-l-akram.
Meaning: My Lord, forgive and have mercy. You are the Most Mighty, the Most Generous.
Reported from Companions, not from the Prophet ﷺ · Musannaf Ibn Abi Shaybah (Book of Hajj); al-Bayhaqi, al-Sunan al-Kubra 5/95

Sending blessings on the Prophet ﷺ (full)
اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ، كَمَا صَلَّيْتَ عَلَى آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ، اللَّهُمَّ بَارِكْ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ، كَمَا بَارَكْتَ عَلَى آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ
Allāhumma ṣalli ‘alā Muḥammadin wa ‘alā āli Muḥammad, kamā ṣallayta ‘alā āli Ibrāhīm, innaka ḥamīdun majīd. Allāhumma bārik ‘alā Muḥammadin wa ‘alā āli Muḥammad, kamā bārakta ‘alā āli Ibrāhīm, innaka ḥamīdun majīd.
Meaning: O Allah, send prayers on Muhammad and the family of Muhammad, as You sent prayers on the family of Ibrahim; You are Praiseworthy, Glorious. O Allah, bless Muhammad and the family of Muhammad, as You blessed the family of Ibrahim; You are Praiseworthy, Glorious.
Sahih al-Bukhari 3370
7Travel: leaving, journey, return
Leaving your home
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ، تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى اللَّهِ، لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ
Bismillāh, tawakkaltu ‘ala-llāh, lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā billāh.
Meaning: In the name of Allah. I put my trust in Allah. There is no might nor power except with Allah.
Sunan Abi Dawud 5095 (sahih)
Setting off on the journey (full travel dua)
اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ. سُبْحَانَ الَّذِي سَخَّرَ لَنَا هَذَا وَمَا كُنَّا لَهُ مُقْرِنِينَ، وَإِنَّا إِلَى رَبِّنَا لَمُنْقَلِبُونَ. اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّا نَسْأَلُكَ فِي سَفَرِنَا هَذَا الْبِرَّ وَالتَّقْوَى، وَمِنَ الْعَمَلِ مَا تَرْضَى. اللَّهُمَّ هَوِّنْ عَلَيْنَا سَفَرَنَا هَذَا وَاطْوِ عَنَّا بُعْدَهُ. اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ الصَّاحِبُ فِي السَّفَرِ، وَالْخَلِيفَةُ فِي الْأَهْلِ. اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ وَعْثَاءِ السَّفَرِ، وَكَآبَةِ الْمَنْظَرِ، وَسُوءِ الْمُنْقَلَبِ فِي الْمَالِ وَالْأَهْلِ
Allāhu akbar (×3). Subḥāna-lladhī sakhkhara lanā hādhā wa mā kunnā lahū muqrinīn, wa innā ilā rabbinā la-munqalibūn. Allāhumma innā nas’aluka fī safarinā hādha-l-birra wa-t-taqwā, wa mina-l-‘amali mā tarḍā. Allāhumma hawwin ‘alaynā safaranā hādhā wa-ṭwi ‘annā bu‘dah. Allāhumma anta-ṣ-ṣāḥibu fi-s-safar, wa-l-khalīfatu fi-l-ahl. Allāhumma innī a‘ūdhu bika min wa‘thā’i-s-safar, wa ka’ābati-l-manẓar, wa sū’i-l-munqalabi fi-l-māli wa-l-ahl.
Meaning: Allah is the Greatest (three times). Glory be to the One who has placed this at our service; we could not have done it ourselves, and to our Lord we will surely return. O Allah, we ask You on this journey for goodness and piety, and for deeds that please You. O Allah, make this journey easy for us and shorten its distance. O Allah, You are the Companion on the journey and the One who looks after the family. O Allah, I seek refuge in You from the hardship of travel, from distressing sights, and from an evil return to wealth and family.
Sahih Muslim 1342
Arriving at a new place (hotel, rest stop)
أَعُوذُ بِكَلِمَاتِ اللَّهِ التَّامَّاتِ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ
A‘ūdhu bi-kalimāti-llāhi-t-tāmmāti min sharri mā khalaq.
Meaning: I seek refuge in the perfect words of Allah from the evil of what He has created.
Sahih Muslim 2708
Returning home (add to the travel dua)
آيِبُونَ، تَائِبُونَ، عَابِدُونَ، لِرَبِّنَا حَامِدُونَ
Āyibūna, tā’ibūna, ‘ābidūna, li-rabbinā ḥāmidūn.
Meaning: We return, repenting, worshipping, and praising our Lord.
Sahih Muslim 1342
8Suggested for Tawaf & Sa‘i
Suggestions, not requirementsThese are authentic duas from the Qur’an and Sunnah that suit Tawaf and Sa‘i. None of them is fixed to a round or lap. Pick any, in any order, or use your own words.
The dua of Ibrahim and Isma‘il, building the Ka‘bah
رَبَّنَا تَقَبَّلْ مِنَّا إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ
Rabbanā taqabbal minnā, innaka anta-s-samī‘u-l-‘alīm.
Meaning: Our Lord, accept this from us. You are the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing.
Qur’an 2:127
Asking forgiveness (the dua of Adam and his wife)
رَبَّنَا ظَلَمْنَا أَنْفُسَنَا وَإِنْ لَمْ تَغْفِرْ لَنَا وَتَرْحَمْنَا لَنَكُونَنَّ مِنَ الْخَاسِرِينَ
Rabbanā ẓalamnā anfusanā wa in lam taghfir lanā wa tarḥamnā la-nakūnanna mina-l-khāsirīn.
Meaning: Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves. If You do not forgive us and have mercy on us, we will surely be among the losers.
Qur’an 7:23
The best way to ask forgiveness (Sayyid al-Istighfar)
اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ رَبِّي، لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ، خَلَقْتَنِي وَأَنَا عَبْدُكَ، وَأَنَا عَلَى عَهْدِكَ وَوَعْدِكَ مَا اسْتَطَعْتُ، أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا صَنَعْتُ، أَبُوءُ لَكَ بِنِعْمَتِكَ عَلَيَّ، وَأَبُوءُ لَكَ بِذَنْبِي، فَاغْفِرْ لِي، فَإِنَّهُ لَا يَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوبَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ
Allāhumma anta rabbī, lā ilāha illā ant, khalaqtanī wa ana ‘abduk, wa ana ‘alā ‘ahdika wa wa‘dika mastaṭa‘t, a‘ūdhu bika min sharri mā ṣana‘t, abū’u laka bi-ni‘matika ‘alayy, wa abū’u laka bi-dhanbī, faghfir lī, fa-innahū lā yaghfiru-dh-dhunūba illā ant.
Meaning: O Allah, You are my Lord; none has the right to be worshipped but You. You created me and I am Your servant. I keep Your covenant and promise as best I can. I seek refuge in You from the evil I have done. I acknowledge Your favour on me, and I acknowledge my sin, so forgive me, for none forgives sins but You.
Sahih al-Bukhari 6306
Asking for pardon
اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنِّي
Allāhumma innaka ‘afuwwun tuḥibbu-l-‘afwa fa‘fu ‘annī.
Meaning: O Allah, You are the One who pardons, and You love to pardon, so pardon me.
Sunan Ibn Majah 3850 (sahih); also Jami‘ at-Tirmidhi 3513
For guidance and a firm heart
رَبَّنَا لَا تُزِغْ قُلُوبَنَا بَعْدَ إِذْ هَدَيْتَنَا وَهَبْ لَنَا مِنْ لَدُنْكَ رَحْمَةً إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ الْوَهَّابُ
Rabbanā lā tuzigh qulūbanā ba‘da idh hadaytanā wa hab lanā min ladunka raḥmah, innaka anta-l-wahhāb.
Meaning: Our Lord, do not let our hearts turn away after You have guided us, and grant us mercy from Yourself. You are the Giver.
Qur’an 3:8
O Turner of hearts
يَا مُقَلِّبَ الْقُلُوبِ ثَبِّتْ قَلْبِي عَلَى دِينِكَ
Yā muqalliba-l-qulūb, thabbit qalbī ‘alā dīnik.
Meaning: O Turner of hearts, keep my heart firm on Your religion.
Jami‘ at-Tirmidhi 2140 (hasan)
Guidance, piety, chastity and contentment
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْهُدَى وَالتُّقَى وَالْعَفَافَ وَالْغِنَى
Allāhumma innī as’aluka-l-hudā wa-t-tuqā wa-l-‘afāfa wa-l-ghinā.
Meaning: O Allah, I ask You for guidance, piety, chastity, and freedom from need.
Sahih Muslim 2721

Rabbi-rḥamhumā kamā rabbayānī ṣaghīrā.
Meaning: My Lord, have mercy on them both, as they raised me when I was small.

Rabbanā hab lanā min azwājinā wa dhurriyyātinā qurrata a‘yunin waj‘alnā li-l-muttaqīna imāmā.
Meaning: Our Lord, give us joy in our spouses and children, and make us leaders of the God-conscious.
Qur’an 25:74
The dua of Ibrahim for prayer and family
رَبِّ اجْعَلْنِي مُقِيمَ الصَّلَاةِ وَمِنْ ذُرِّيَّتِي رَبَّنَا وَتَقَبَّلْ دُعَاءِ
Rabbi-j‘alnī muqīma-ṣ-ṣalāti wa min dhurriyyatī, rabbanā wa taqabbal du‘ā’.
