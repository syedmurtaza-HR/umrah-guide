How this guide handles differences

The main steps follow widely held positions. Where recognised schools of fiqh differ, we say so with a Scholars differ label and explain here. If you follow a particular school or scholar, follow them.

Scholarly notes
Is Umrah obligatory?Shafi‘i and Hanbali scholars: obligatory once in a lifetime for those able. Hanafi and Maliki scholars: a strongly emphasised Sunnah.
“Essential” actsSchools differ on which acts are pillars (rukn) and which are obligations (wajib). The guide calls all four “essential” because every pilgrim performs them.
Wudu for TawafThe majority hold it is a condition. Hanafi scholars hold it is obligatory but not a condition. If wudu breaks mid-Tawaf, views differ on continuing or restarting.
Ihram prayerMany scholars recommend 2 rak‘ahs before ihram. Others hold there is no prayer specific to ihram, and one enters ihram after an obligatory prayer if it is time.
Two rak‘ahs after TawafThe majority: Sunnah. Hanafi scholars: wajib. Views also differ on praying them at disliked times.
Minimum hair to cut (men)Views include: the whole head, most of the head, a quarter of the head, or three hairs. Shaving, or shortening from the whole head, satisfies all views.
Meeqat for air travellersMost scholars: enter ihram before the aircraft crosses the Meeqat. Some consider Jeddah a valid place for certain arrivals.
Farewell Tawaf after UmrahObligatory after Hajj. For Umrah, some scholars say obligatory, others recommended.
Women’s face covering in ihramNiqab is not worn. Many allow lowering a cloth near non-mahram men; views differ on whether it may touch the face. (The report in Abu Dawud 1833 is graded weak by al-Albani.)
Menstruation and the MasjidMost scholars: a menstruating woman does not stay in the Masjid. Some permit it.
What we removed, and why
Claims found in common booklets that we could not trace to an authentic report are left out: e.g. guaranteed acceptance of every dua at the first sight of the Ka‘bah, and set duas for each round of Tawaf.
Official rules, checked Sept 2026
Health (Saudi Ministry of Health, Umrah 1447H/2026)Meningitis vaccine required for all Umrah pilgrims: ACYW/ACYWX conjugate within 5 years, or polysaccharide within 3 years, at least 10 days before arrival. Polio and yellow fever only for listed countries. COVID-19 only for 65+, pregnant women and chronic illness.
Permits (Ministry of Hajj and Umrah)Umrah permits are booked in the Nusuk app. Umrah is possible on an Umrah visa or a visit/tourist visa.
Bookings (Ministry of Hajj and Umrah / Nusuk)Umrah visas require a confirmed licensed-hotel booking recorded in Nusuk Masar (announced June 2025, for 1447H onward). Travel sources report that ground transport must also be pre-booked for 1448H; confirm with your agent. Rawdah visits need a free Nusuk permit with a fixed time slot.
Haramain trainOperated by Saudi Arabia Railways. Stations: Makkah, Jeddah (Al-Sulimaniyah), King Abdulaziz International Airport, King Abdullah Economic City and Madinah. Makkah to Madinah about 2 hours.
WomenSince October 2022 Saudi rules no longer require a mahram for women performing Umrah.
Emergency911 is the unified emergency number in the Makkah region (Saudi Press Agency, June 2026).
Not officially confirmedPassport validity of 6 months is widely stated by airlines and agents; we could not find it on an official Saudi page.
