# Shahid: Witness the Journey (free Umrah guide, offline web app)

A free, step-by-step Umrah companion. Static files only; no server code, no accounts, no tracking.

## Put it online with GitHub Pages (free)

1. Create a new **public** repository on GitHub, e.g. `umrah-guide`.
2. Upload **everything in this folder** to the root of the repository (keep the `assets` and `icons` folders, and the hidden `.nojekyll` file).
3. In the repository go to **Settings → Pages**. Under *Build and deployment* choose **Deploy from a branch**, branch **main**, folder **/ (root)**, then **Save**.
4. After a minute the guide is live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/umrah-guide/`.

All paths are relative, so it works both at a project address (`/umrah-guide/`) and on a custom domain.

## Install on a phone

- **Android (Chrome):** open the address, then tap **Install** on the home screen banner, or ⋮ → *Install app*.
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the address, tap **Share** → *Add to Home Screen*.

Open it once while online; after that every page works with no internet.

## What is stored on the phone

Only in the browser's local storage on that phone, never sent anywhere:
checklist ticks, the dua list, the Tawaf and Sa‘i counters, the last step opened, "My info" fields and the text size.
"Start a new Umrah" in the Menu clears progress but keeps "My info" and the dua list.

## Companion voice

The guide speaks to the reader as a companion. The wording lives in a layer on top of the design text: `voice/en_voice.json` (English, by text id) and `ur_13_voice.py` (matching Urdu), applied by `build.py`. Rulings, numbers, quotes and citations were deliberately left unchanged.

## My trip: month and country (and notes for the future app)

First-visit setup is three short questions after the language: **who is travelling**, **when** (the next 12 months, or "not sure yet") and **where from** (Pakistan, UAE, living in Saudi Arabia, UK, another country). Answers are saved on the phone only.

Everything personal is driven by data, so a future Android/iOS app can reuse it unchanged:

| Stored key (localStorage `ug:`) | Values |
|---|---|
| `lang` | `en` or `ur` |
| `who` | list of `man`, `woman`, `couple`, `elderly`, `babies`, `kids` |
| `when` | `YYYY-MM`, or empty for not sure |
| `from` | `pk`, `ae`, `sa`, `gb`, `other` |

- `assets/trip-data.json` holds monthly climate for Makkah and Madinah, dated season events (Ramadan, end of the Umrah season, the closure around Hajj, reopening) and per-country advice, each in English and Urdu with its source. **Update the event dates every season.**
- Page content is tagged with `data-for` (traveller type) and `data-from` (country). `build.py` turns these into `assets/profile.css`.
- The My trip page (`trip.html`) and the Home "My trip" card are filled in from this data.

## Personalised guide (who is travelling)

After choosing a language, readers pick who is travelling: man on his own, woman (alone or in a women's group), couple, with elderly parents, with babies or young children, with older children. Content marked `data-for="men"`, `women`, `elderly`, `babies`, `kids` or `couple` is then hidden when it does not apply. A bar on each page shows the choice, with **Change** and **Show all**. The hiding rules are generated by build.py into `assets/profile.css`.

## English and Urdu

Every page exists twice: `step5.html` (English) and `step5-ur.html` (Urdu, right-to-left, Nastaliq font).
On the first visit the reader picks a language; the choice is saved on the phone and every page opens in that language.
Switch any time with the English / اردو buttons (Home, Menu, and the bar under each page title). The spoken key points follow the page language.
The Urdu text was drafted with AI help and should be checked by an Urdu-speaking scholar before wide sharing.

## Key points (listen) in Urdu and English

Each page has a **اہم باتیں سنیں / Key points (listen)** button. It speaks a short summary of what matters on that page and what to do, not the whole page. Urdu is the default; English is one tap away.

- The summaries (Urdu and English) are stored in `assets/narration.json`; edit them there.
- **Recorded voice (recommended):** put MP3 files in `assets/audio/`, named `<page>-ur.mp3` or `<page>-en.mp3` (for example `step5-ur.mp3`). The app plays a recording when one exists and falls back to the phone's own voice otherwise. See the separate recording script for the exact text and file names.
- **Phone voice:** the app picks the softest-sounding voice on the phone and speaks slightly slowly. Many Android phones have an Urdu voice (Settings › Text-to-speech › Google › Install voice data › Urdu). Many iPhones do not; the app then shows the key points on screen.

## Updating content

Edit the HTML pages, then change anything in `service-worker.js` (for example the `VERSION` value) so phones download the new version. Users see "Guide updated" and get it next time they open the app.

## Before publishing

- Have the content reviewed by a qualified scholar and fill in the `[REVIEWING SCHOLAR / INSTITUTION]`, `[GUIDE WEB ADDRESS]` and `[QR CODE]` placeholders.
- The small speaker buttons on dua cards are for a future Arabic recitation; other languages and night mode show "coming soon" messages.
- Saudi rules (visas, Nusuk, vaccines) were checked in September 2026; re-check each season.
