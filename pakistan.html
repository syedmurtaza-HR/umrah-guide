Vaccines and your certificate
No certificate, no boarding passAirlines flying from Pakistan will not let you board for Saudi Arabia without the right vaccination proof. Please sort this out early.

Polio rule: Saudi General Authority of Civil Aviation circular, reported by Dawn, 14 Feb 2025 · Meningitis: Saudi Ministry of Health, Umrah health requirements · NADRA certificate: Border Health Services notice of 7 Sept 2026, reported by Dawn, 12 Sept 2026, and Daily Pakistan, 14 Sept 2026

Ihram on the flight
  1. 1
    Your flight to Jeddah crosses the Meeqat while you are still in the air.

    So you must be in ihram before that point, not after landing.

    Must
  2. 2
    Men: put on your ihram sheets at home or at the airport.

    Trust me, it is much easier than changing in a small aircraft toilet. Wearing the clothes early is fine; ihram begins with the intention.

  3. 3
    When the crew announces the Meeqat, make your intention and begin the Talbiyah.

    Not sure? Just ask the crew when the plane will pass the Meeqat.

  4. 4
    Flying to Madinah first? Then no ihram on this flight.

    You will enter ihram at Dhul-Hulayfah (Abyar ‘Ali) when you leave Madinah for Makkah.

Jeddah as a Meeqat?Most scholars say you must be in ihram before the plane passes the Meeqat. Some allow Jeddah for certain routes. Follow the scholar you trust. Step 2

Lost your passport?
  1. 1
    Don't panic. Tell your group leader or agent, and keep proof of the loss.

    For example, a police report.

  2. 2
    Then go to the Consulate in Jeddah for an Emergency Travel Document.

    ETD section, Room 13, ground floor. No appointment needed. Sunday to Thursday, 9 am to 4 pm.

  3. 3
    Take:

    the application form, 2 recent photos (white or blue background), a copy of your lost passport, a copy of your CNIC, and proof of the loss.

Before you leave homeSave photos of your passport, visa, CNIC and vaccine card on your phone, and email them to yourself too.

Money & phone
  • Cash limit leaving Pakistan: the State Bank of Pakistan set USD 5,000 per visit and USD 30,000 a year for adults, and half that for children. Please check the current limit with your bank before you fly.
  • Carry some Saudi riyals for small shops and taxis, plus a bank card that works abroad. Let your bank know you are travelling.
  • Saudi SIM or eSIM: bring your original passport. You can buy a SIM at airport counters and operator shops, and every SIM must be registered to your identity.
  • Before you fly: install Nusuk and this guide, and open every page once so I can stay with you offline.
