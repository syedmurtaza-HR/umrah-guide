Nusuk, step by step
  1. 1
    Download Nusuk only from your official app store.

    It is the official platform of the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah. Please stay away from look-alike apps and websites.

  2. 2
    Create your account.

    Keep your passport and visa details handy.

  3. 3
    Book your Umrah permit.

    Choose your date and time. You cannot enter for Umrah before your permit time, so plan around it.

  4. 4
    Visiting Madinah? Book your Rawdah permit too.

    It is free. Men and women have separate times.

  5. 5
    Keep your permits ready to show.

    Take a screenshot, just in case you have no signal.

Health in the heat
  • Drink often, before you feel thirsty. You'll find Zamzam all around the Masjid.
  • Use an umbrella in the sun (allowed in ihram), and try to go at cooler times.
  • Feet: wear sandals you have already walked in. Carry plasters, just in case of blisters.
  • Chafing (men in ihram): use an unscented anti-chafe cream or petroleum jelly. It really helps.
  • Diabetes: keep snacks and your glucose kit with you; long walks can lower your sugar.
Signs of heat exhaustionDizziness, headache, nausea, heavy sweating or confusion. Stop, get into the shade, drink water, and ask for help. Don't push on. In an emergency in Makkah, call 911.
Practical tips
  • Shoes: carry them in a small bag instead of leaving them at the door. It saves you a long search later.
  • Carry light: your phone, water, this guide, a little cash and your hotel card. Leave valuables at the hotel.
  • Crowds change with the season and prayer times. Ask your group leader when it is usually calmer.
  • Stay charged: a power bank keeps this guide, your counters and your Nusuk permits working all day.
Avoid
  • Unofficial visa, Nusuk or train websites that ask for payment.
  • Leaving the vaccine to the last week. It must be at least 10 days before you arrive.
  • Wearing brand-new shoes on the day of Umrah.

Checked September 2026: vaccine rules, Saudi Ministry of Health (Umrah 1447H); permits and Nusuk Masar, Ministry of Hajj and Umrah. Rules change each season; check again before booking.
