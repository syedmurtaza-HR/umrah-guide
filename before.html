Nusuk, step by step
  1. 1
    Download Nusuk only from the official app store.

    It is the official platform of the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah. Avoid look-alike apps and websites.

  2. 2
    Create your account.

    You will need your passport and visa details.

  3. 3
    Book your Umrah permit.

    Choose the date and time. You cannot enter for Umrah before your permit time.

  4. 4
    Visiting Madinah? Book a Rawdah permit.

    It is free. Men and women have separate times.

  5. 5
    Keep your permits easy to show.

    Take a screenshot in case you have no signal.

Health in the heat
  • Drink often, before you feel thirsty. Zamzam is available all around the Masjid.
  • Use an umbrella in the sun (allowed in ihram) and go at cooler times.
  • Feet: wear sandals you have walked in before. Carry plasters for blisters.
  • Chafing (men in ihram): use an unscented anti-chafe cream or petroleum jelly.
  • Diabetes: carry snacks and your glucose kit; long walks can lower your sugar.
Signs of heat exhaustionDizziness, headache, nausea, heavy sweating or confusion. Stop, get into shade, drink water, and ask for help. In an emergency in Makkah, call 911.
Practical tips
  • Shoes: carry them in a small bag instead of leaving them at the door. It saves a long search afterwards.
  • Carry light: phone, water, this guide, a little cash, your hotel card. Leave valuables at the hotel.
  • Crowds change with the season and prayer times. Ask your group leader when it is usually calmer.
  • Charge up: a power bank keeps your guide, counters and Nusuk permits working all day.
Avoid
  • Unofficial visa, Nusuk or train websites that ask for payment.
  • Leaving the vaccine to the last week. It must be at least 10 days before arrival.
  • Brand-new shoes on the day of Umrah.

Checked September 2026: vaccine rules, Saudi Ministry of Health (Umrah 1447H); permits and Nusuk Masar, Ministry of Hajj and Umrah. Rules change each season; check again before booking.
